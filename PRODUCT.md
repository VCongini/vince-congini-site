# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and technical leaders evaluating Vincent Congini for senior engineering or leadership roles. They're scanning quickly on desktop and mobile to judge technical competence, communication clarity, and professional fit. Secondary audience: peers and collaborators exploring his work and capabilities.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and resume site for Vincent Congini, a Senior Front End Tech Lead. The site exists to convert professional interest into contact or interview opportunities by presenting experience, projects, and capabilities with clarity and credibility. Success looks like a visitor who leaves confident that Vince is a serious, capable engineer worth talking to.

## Brand Personality

Technically credible, understated confidence, precise. The voice is direct and professional without being corporate. It demonstrates craft through execution, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap/template portfolio sites with stock layouts and default spacing
- Overly flashy animated developer portfolios (three.js hero scenes, particle backgrounds)
- SaaS-cream startup aesthetic (ivory backgrounds, pastel accents, rounded everything)
- Cookie-cutter dark-mode developer sites with neon accents

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — the site itself should demonstrate the front-end craft and attention to detail that Vince claims professionally.
2. **Substance over spectacle** — let the work and experience speak; design supports content rather than competing with it.
3. **Expert confidence** — decisive visual choices, no hedging or visual clutter. Every element earns its place.
4. **Respectful of time** — visitors are busy professionals scanning for signal. Information hierarchy should reward both skimmers and deep readers.
5. **Quietly distinctive** — memorable without being loud. The site should feel authored, not generated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` already respected with reduced animation fallbacks. Dark mode supported via `prefers-color-scheme`. Semantic HTML with skip links, ARIA labels, and keyboard navigation already in place.
